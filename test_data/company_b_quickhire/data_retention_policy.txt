QUICKHIRE INC
DATA RETENTION POLICY
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Approved by: Legal & Compliance
Last Reviewed: December 2023

---

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes data retention and disposal standards for QuickHire Inc. It applies to all data collected, processed, or stored by QuickHire in the course of its business operations, including data held in company systems, third-party SaaS platforms, and cloud storage.

All employees, contractors, and third-party service providers with access to QuickHire data are required to follow this policy.

---

2. GENERAL RETENTION PRINCIPLES

QuickHire retains data only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law or regulation. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted or de-identified in accordance with this policy and using approved methods.

Data should not be retained indefinitely "just in case." Retention periods in this policy represent the maximum time data should be kept absent a legal hold or active business need.

---

3. RETENTION SCHEDULE BY DATA CATEGORY

3.1 Human Resources Records

- Employee personnel files: 7 years from date of termination
- Payroll and compensation records: 7 years
- Benefits enrollment records: 7 years from plan year end
- I-9 employment eligibility forms: 3 years from hire date or 1 year from termination, whichever is later
- Job applications and resumes (unsuccessful candidates): 2 years from application date
- Interview notes and evaluation records: 2 years from position close date
- Offer letters and employment contracts: Duration of employment + 7 years

3.2 Financial Records

- Accounts payable and receivable: 5 years
- General ledger and financial statements: 7 years
- Expense reports and receipts: 5 years
- Tax records and filings: 7 years
- Bank statements and reconciliation records: 5 years
- Customer contracts and invoices: 5 years after contract expiration

3.3 Legal and Compliance Records

- Litigation files and legal holds: Duration of matter + 7 years
- Regulatory filings and correspondence: 7 years
- Insurance policies and claims: 10 years
- Board minutes and corporate governance documents: Permanent

3.4 Customer and Platform Data

- Customer account records: Duration of contract + 5 years
- Platform usage logs: 2 years
- Customer support records and tickets: 3 years after ticket close
- Customer communications: 3 years

3.5 IT and Security Records

- System access logs: 1 year
- Security incident records: 5 years
- Backup files: 90 days (rolling)
- Email: 3 years (standard), indefinite for executives and legal hold subjects

---

4. AI SYSTEM DATA

QuickHire operates AI-assisted tools as part of its recruiting platform. Data outputs generated by these systems are subject to the following retention:

- AI system outputs (candidate scores, rankings, screening decisions): Retained for 90 days for quality assurance purposes, then deleted.
- Candidate resumes submitted through the platform are retained per the schedule in Section 3.1 (2 years for unsuccessful candidates).

---

5. DATA DISPOSAL

When data reaches the end of its retention period, it must be disposed of using the following methods:

- Electronic records: Secure deletion using approved tools (IT team maintains approved tool list). Cloud storage: deletion via provider console with deletion confirmation retained for 30 days.
- Physical records: Cross-cut shredding or engagement of a certified document destruction vendor.
- Records subject to a legal hold must not be deleted until the hold is lifted by Legal.

---

6. LEGAL HOLDS

When QuickHire becomes aware of pending or anticipated litigation, government investigation, or regulatory inquiry, Legal will issue a legal hold notice to relevant employees and system owners. All data potentially relevant to the matter must be preserved regardless of its standard retention period until the hold is lifted.

Current legal holds are tracked by the Legal department. Questions about legal holds should be directed to legal@quickhire.io.

---

7. THIRD-PARTY AND VENDOR DATA

Data shared with third-party vendors must be governed by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that includes retention and deletion requirements consistent with this policy. Procurement is responsible for ensuring DPAs are in place before data is shared with vendors.

---

8. POLICY EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Legal & Compliance team. Approved exceptions are logged and reviewed annually.

---

9. POLICY VIOLATIONS

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action. Intentional destruction of records subject to a legal hold may result in termination and may expose QuickHire to legal liability.

---

10. POLICY REVIEW

This policy will be reviewed annually by Legal & Compliance. The next scheduled review is December 2024.
