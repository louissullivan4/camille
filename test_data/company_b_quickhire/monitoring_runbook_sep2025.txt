1. OVERVIEW

This runbook documents the operational monitoring setup for the Resume Screener AI (v2.3.1) deployed on AWS SageMaker. It is intended for the ML Platform team and on-call engineers.

---

2. MONITORING INFRASTRUCTURE

All production metrics are collected via Amazon CloudWatch. Dashboards are available in the ml-platform AWS account under the "resume-screener-prod" dashboard group.

Metrics collected:
- Request latency (p50, p95, p99)
- Error rate (% of failed scoring requests)
- Throughput (requests per hour)
- Score distribution (histogram of output scores, updated daily)
- Advancement rate (% of resumes above threshold, by day and requisition type)

---

3. PERFORMANCE THRESHOLDS AND ALERTS

The following CloudWatch alarms are configured:

| Metric | Warning threshold | Critical threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Error rate | > 0.5% | > 2% | Page on-call engineer |
| p95 latency | > 800ms | > 2000ms | Page on-call engineer |
| Advancement rate (daily) | < 15% or > 60% | < 10% or > 75% | Notify ML team |
| Score distribution shift | Std dev change > 0.1 vs 30-day baseline | Std dev change > 0.2 | Notify ML team |

Alerts are sent to: #ml-alerts Slack channel and paged via PagerDuty for critical thresholds.

---

4. MODEL PERFORMANCE MONITORING

The ML team reviews model performance on a monthly basis using a sample of production decisions with ground-truth outcomes (provided by customers who share hiring outcome data).

Metrics reviewed monthly:
- Precision, Recall, F1 on available ground-truth sample
- Advancement rate consistency across demographic segments (spot-check)
- Customer complaint patterns related to screening results

Performance review findings are logged in Confluence under ml-team/model-reviews.

---

5. RETRAINING TRIGGERS

Model retraining is initiated under the following conditions:

- F1 score on monthly ground-truth sample drops below 0.75 (confirmed over two consecutive monthly reviews)
- Advancement rate drift: daily advancement rate falls outside 15–60% range for more than 5 consecutive business days
- Significant distribution shift in score histogram (std dev change > 0.2 sustained over 7 days)
- Product team or legal team escalation requesting retraining review

Retraining requests are logged as tickets in the ml-team Jira project. Retraining process follows the ML model deployment checklist (see ml-team/deployment-checklist in Confluence).

---

6. INCIDENT RESPONSE FOR MODEL ISSUES

If a model-related incident is suspected (e.g., incorrect scoring behavior, customer complaint about results):

1. Page the ML on-call via PagerDuty (use tag: resume-screener-prod)
2. Preserve logs for the affected time period (do not delete CloudWatch logs)
3. Escalate to Talent Operations if customer-facing impact is confirmed
4. Consult the IT Incident Response Policy for escalation beyond the ML team

Note: Rollback to the previous model version (v2.2.0) is possible but requires coordination with the ML team and Talent Operations due to scoring threshold changes between versions. Rollback is not self-service — contact priya@quickhire.io.

---

7. KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- Performance monitoring relies on ground-truth data voluntarily shared by customers. Coverage is approximately 40% of production volume.
- Drift detection is based on output score distribution, not on model internals. This may miss some forms of semantic drift.
- No automated retraining pipeline exists. All retraining is manual and initiated by the ML team.
