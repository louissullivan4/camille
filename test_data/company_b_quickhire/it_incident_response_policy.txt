QUICKHIRE INC
IT INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: August 1, 2025
Approved by: Legal & Compliance, IT Security

---

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes QuickHire's procedures for identifying, responding to, and recovering from IT security incidents and service disruptions. It applies to all systems, applications, and data assets operated by QuickHire.

---

2. INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION

Incidents are classified into three severity levels:

SEVERITY 1 — CRITICAL
- Customer data breach or unauthorized access to personal data
- Complete service outage affecting all customers
- Ransomware or destructive malware
- Regulatory breach with mandatory notification requirements

SEVERITY 2 — HIGH
- Partial service outage affecting a subset of customers
- Suspected unauthorized access to internal systems
- Significant performance degradation affecting customer operations
- Potential data loss (not yet confirmed)

SEVERITY 3 — MEDIUM/LOW
- Internal system outages with no customer impact
- Minor performance degradation
- Isolated user access issues
- Policy violations without immediate security impact

---

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCEDURE

3.1 Detection and Reporting

Any employee who observes or suspects an IT incident must report it immediately to:
- Security team: security@quickhire.io
- PagerDuty: use the "IT-Security" service tag for Severity 1/2

3.2 Containment

- On-call engineer performs initial triage within 30 minutes of Severity 1 page
- Incident commander assigned for Severity 1 and 2 incidents
- Affected systems isolated or placed in read-only mode as appropriate
- Evidence preservation: logs must be preserved before any remediation actions

3.3 Investigation

- Root cause analysis conducted for all Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents
- Timeline reconstruction and impact assessment documented
- External forensics engaged for incidents involving personal data

3.4 Notification

REGULATORY NOTIFICATION — Severity 1 incidents involving personal data:
- Legal & Compliance team notified within 1 hour of classification
- If personal data breach is confirmed, Legal assesses notification obligations under applicable law
- GDPR: supervisory authority notification within 72 hours if applicable
- State privacy laws (CCPA, etc.): notification per legal assessment
- Legal counsel engaged for all Severity 1 personal data incidents

Customer notification:
- Customers affected by Severity 1 service outages notified within 4 hours via status page and email
- Personal data incidents: notification timeline per Legal assessment

3.5 Recovery

- Service restoration plan documented before any recovery actions for Severity 1
- Stakeholder sign-off required before declaring incident resolved
- Post-incident monitoring period: minimum 72 hours for Severity 1

---

4. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW

A post-incident review (PIR) is required for all Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents.

PIR must be completed within 5 business days of incident closure and include:
- Timeline of events
- Root cause analysis
- Impact assessment (customers affected, data at risk)
- Actions taken during response
- Corrective actions and owners
- Process improvements

PIR documents are retained for 5 years and shared with Legal & Compliance.

---

5. AI SYSTEM CONSIDERATIONS

This policy covers all IT systems operated by QuickHire including AI-powered tools such as the Resume Screener AI. However, this policy does not address AI-specific incident scenarios such as:
- Model bias incidents
- Incorrect AI predictions causing harm to candidates
- Regulatory inquiries specific to automated decision-making

AI-specific incident response procedures are under development by the ML and Legal teams. In the interim, any AI-related incidents should be escalated using this policy's Severity 1/2 procedures with the incident commander looping in the ML team lead and Legal immediately.
