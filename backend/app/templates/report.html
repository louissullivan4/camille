5. Methodology

Scoring model: Each of 8 governance dimensions is scored 0–100 using deterministic rules applied to structured data extracted from submitted governance documents. Dimension scores are weighted and summed to produce the overall score.

Dimension weights: Model Inventory 15% · Human Oversight 20% · Bias & Fairness 15% · Data Governance 10% · Incident Response 15% · Monitoring & Drift 10% · Regulatory Compliance 10% · Third-Party Risk 5%.

Risk tiers: Low (75–100) · Medium (50–74) · High (25–49) · Critical (0–24).

Staleness penalty: Governance documents older than 12 months receive a scoring penalty (13–24 months: 0.75×; >24 months: 0.50×) on quality sub-scores.

Missing documentation: Dimensions with no submitted documentation are treated as critical risk signals and scored at 0.

Extraction: Governance data is extracted from submitted documents using Claude (Anthropic) with structured tool-use schemas. All extractions are evidence-linked to source documents.
